## Publication-date offset check (NBK vs ECB EUR/USD)

ECB reference rates, 2005-01-03..2009-12-09 (the file reachable by the downloader stops in Feb 2010 and holds sparse/placeholder rows after 2009-12; 8 such rows rejected). Correlation of daily changes in log(EUR/KZT / USD/KZT) from NBK with log EUR/USD from ECB when the NBK date is shifted back by k calendar days to obtain the market date:

| shift k (days) | matched days | corr of daily changes |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 1252 | -0.029 |
| 1 | 1252 | 0.811 |
| 2 | 1252 | 0.382 |

Best alignment: shift = 1 day(s). NBK rates are therefore assigned to the previous business day (`market_date`). The ECB fix is a 14:15 CET snapshot while the exact NBK fixing time was not verified, so the correlation is well below 1 even at the right shift; the point is the clear contrast between shifts.
